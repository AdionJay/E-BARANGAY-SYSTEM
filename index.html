About E-Barangay System

This is a digital platform designed to serve the community through transparent, efficient, and accessible government services.

Mission

Deliver fast, secure, and resident-centered barangay services through a modern digital platform.

Vision

A progressive system where every resident can access documents, records, and assistance online with ease.

Commitment

Accurate records, verified residency, accountable payments, and official documents you can trust.
